About Me

I am a Physics student with a strong interest in Astronomy and Astrophysics, particularly in areas involving the interstellar medium, radiative processes, solar physics and space science.

My academic and research experiences have allowed me to work with observational data, computational modelling, scientific programming and data analysis.

Alongside research, I am interested in scientific communication, student-led discussions and creating spaces where students can freely exchange ideas.

Projects

Dust Continuum Radiative Transfer

Modelling dust scattering at near-infrared wavelengths using RADMC-3D to study the coreshine phenomenon in molecular clouds.

CME Shock Standoff Distance

Analysis and modelling of CME-driven shocks using remote-sensing and in-situ observations, including coronagraphic and L1 measurements.

Spectral Data Analysis

Experience with astronomical spectral data, FITS files, Python-based analysis and computational methods.

Dialexis

A Student-Led Space for Discussion

Dialexis is a student-led initiative built around conversation, discussion, curiosity and the free exchange of ideas.

It is a space where students can come together, discuss science, share experiences, question ideas and learn from one another.

From scientific discussions to personal experiences, Dialexis aims to encourage an open and thoughtful culture of conversation.
